## Findings (non-VERIFIED items)

### 1. AMBIGUOUS — dropped qualifier on who MAY use OpenID Connect discovery

- Claim (extraction §4 preamble, line 61): "it MAY use OpenID Connect discovery instead"
- Source (§4): "Alternatively, authorization servers implementing OpenID Connect MAY use the OpenID Connect discovery [OpenID.Discovery] document for the same purpose."
- Verdict: AMBIGUOUS. The extraction drops the condition "implementing OpenID Connect" — the MAY is scoped to AS implementations of OpenID Connect, not to any AS. No contradiction results (an AS not implementing OIDC has no OIDC discovery document to publish), but the requirement's subject is narrower than the extraction implies. Minor; suggest wording "an AS implementing OpenID Connect MAY use OIDC discovery instead."
